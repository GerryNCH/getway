GetWay ("we", "us", "our") operates a platform that turns curated and AI-extracted travel content — including TikTok itineraries — into bookable routes. For any privacy question, contact us at getway.smart@gmail.com.

Account data: email address, and any name or profile details you choose to provide.

Usage data: pages viewed, routes saved or booked, links clicked, device type, and approximate location.

Essential cookies keep the site functional; analytics cookies, where enabled, help us understand usage.
